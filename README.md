SNHU CS-250

How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating user stories help with this? 
User stories help make expectations clear and make it easier to understand what the user actually wants. The SNHU Travel project was a good example because the requirements changed when the project moved more toward a wellness travel portal. Since the stories were broken into smaller pieces, we could adjust the work as needed without having to redo everything. Smaller stories also made the work easier because we could change smaller details and still keep moving forward. 

How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work? 
I think it is better to work in smaller chunks and check the work as it goes. Testing along the way also helps make sure things are working the way they are supposed to. One Agile process I would want to keep using is breaking the work into smaller stories. I feel like that makes development more flexible because if something changes or does not work as expected, you can adjust that part instead of having to redo the whole design. 

What does it mean to be a good team member in software development? 
I think communication is one of the biggest parts of being a good team member. Agile gives the team different ways to communicate through Daily Scrums, Sprint Reviews, and Sprint Retrospectives. Good communication also means speaking up when you have a blocker, asking questions when something is not clear, and letting the team know if there is a problem with development or testing. I think that helps the team solve problems earlier instead of waiting until they become bigger issues.
